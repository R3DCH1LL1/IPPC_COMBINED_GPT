IJanuary 2025

The first draft.

One thousand multiple-choice questions, written across the three Core Learning Outcomes that govern the IPPC syllabus — the Malaysian financial system, regulations and conduct, debt and structured products.

Each question came with a single-sentence answer key and an indicative reference to the IPPC Study Text. Nothing fancy. A starting point.

  • 1,000 questions across 20 sets of 50
  • CLO mapping drafted but not yet verified
  • Single-sentence answer keys

IIMay 2025

Expansion, and the first hard sets.

The bank doubled to two thousand questions. Five advanced "Hard Sets" were carved out specifically to drill the trap scenarios — statement-combination items, multi-clause distractors, calculations with intermediate steps.

Set structure was normalised to eighty questions per set, matching the actual examination format.

  • 2,000 questions across 25 sets of 80
  • Hard Sets I–V — 400 advanced traps
  • First Roman-numeral statement-combination items

IIISeptember 2025

A full editorial audit.

Every question reviewed for accuracy against the IPPC Study Text 3rd Edition. Distractor plausibility verified — no arbitrary wrong answers; every option had to map to a real candidate misconception.

Explanations were restructured into the four-part frame the suite still uses today: Why correct, Why each wrong option is wrong, Worked calculation, and Reference clause.

  • Every answer key verified against source text
  • 8,000 distractors reviewed for plausibility
  • Standardised explanation structure introduced

IVFebruary 2026

The quality baseline.

The V173 baseline was established — the last stable milestone before the deep late-stage quality cycle. The study suite had its combined notes, mock test, flashcards, audit and report areas, and deployable structure in place.

The question bank was large-scale and exam-oriented. What remained was a final cycle of question-bank polish, audit evidence, and quality control across all twenty-five sets.

  • V173 baseline sealed — full suite features in place
  • Question bank at 2,000 questions across 25 sets of 80
  • Focus shifted to quality: explanation depth, CLO alignment, stem precision

VMay 2026 — Current edition

The exam-grade question bank.

From V174 to V193, the question bank went through the deepest quality cycle of the project. Flashcards were rebuilt into four source-audited recall decks. Sets 3–25 were then taken through a V187 deep audit that identified 1,554 issues — and six targeted patches followed to close them all.

The V193 final patch corrected Set 19 CLO metadata to restore the 12/36/32 blueprint split. The bank closed at 2,000 questions with zero duplicate stems, zero cut-off explanations, and 218 calculation questions each showing full formula and step-by-step workings.

  • 104 source-audited flashcards across four high-memory recall decks
  • Six targeted patches across Sets 3–25 from V188 to V193
  • 1,160 generic explanations rewritten; 269 topic-mismatch explanations fixed
  • 330 Roman numeral explanations rewritten and properly formatted
  • 566 duplicate and near-duplicate stems resolved
  • 218 calculation questions — all with formula and step-by-step workings
  • Set 19 CLO split restored to 12 / 36 / 32
  • A/B/C/D answer balance held at 460 each across Sets 3–25
· ❦ · Set in Cormorant Garamond & Plus Jakarta Sans. Bound for V193.
