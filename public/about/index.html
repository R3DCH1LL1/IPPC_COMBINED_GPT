IJanuary 2025

The first draft.

One thousand multiple-choice questions, written across the three Core Learning Outcomes that govern the IPPC syllabus — the Malaysian financial system, regulations and conduct, debt and structured products.

Each question came with a single-sentence answer key and an indicative reference to the IPPC Study Text. Nothing fancy. A starting point.

  • 1,000 questions across 20 sets of 50
  • CLO mapping drafted but not yet verified
  • Single-sentence answer keys

IIMay 2025

Expansion, and the first hard sets.

The bank doubled to two thousand questions. Five advanced "Hard Sets" were carved out specifically to drill the trap scenarios — statement-combination items, multi-clause distractors, calculations with intermediate steps.

Set structure was normalised to eighty questions per set, matching the actual examination format.

  • 2,000 questions across 25 sets of 80
  • Hard Sets I–V — 400 advanced traps
  • First Roman-numeral statement-combination items

IIISeptember 2025

A full editorial audit.

Every question reviewed for accuracy against the IPPC Study Text 3rd Edition. Distractor plausibility verified — no arbitrary wrong answers; every option had to map to a real candidate misconception.

Explanations were restructured into the four-part frame the suite still uses today: Why correct, Why each wrong option is wrong, Worked calculation, and Reference clause.

  • Every answer key verified against source text
  • 8,000 distractors reviewed for plausibility
  • Standardised explanation structure introduced

IVFebruary 2026

The quality baseline.

The V134 audit report was published. Explanation language was standardised across all twenty-five sets. CLO mapping reached 98% adherence to the official 12/36/32 blueprint, with the remaining 2% deliberately set aside as boundary cases.

Formal sign-off was recorded for every question. From here onward, the bank had a verifiable provenance.

  • V134 audit report published
  • 98% CLO blueprint adherence
  • 100% explanation coverage with formal sign-off

VMay 2026

The interactive study suite.

The V134 audit dashboard, the animated book-bound interface, the editorial timeline you're reading now — all built on top of the verified question bank. The suite goes live in production with full audit trail, keyboard navigation, theme cross-fade, and accessibility passes for every page.

One hundred and fifty-three editions of foundations laid. The platform was ready for what came next.

  • Interactive audit dashboard + scroll-driven retrospective
  • Book-bound editorial interface across every room
  • Full keyboard shortcuts and accessibility passes

VIMay 2026 — Current edition

2,000 questions, fully rewritten.

From V154 to V167, every question in the bank was brought up to official-source standard. Official Sets 1 and 2 were restored verbatim from the IPPC Mock Examination source. Sets 3 through 25 were rebuilt from the ground up — no artificial openers, no answer-length cueing, no boilerplate explanations.

The final pass added calculation workings: every formula question now shows the formula, clear step-by-step workings, and the final answer. The package was stripped to 4.4 MB — down from 28 MB — by removing build artefacts and duplicate files.

  • Sets 1–2 restored from official IPPC Mock Examination source
  • Sets 3–25 fully rewritten — zero duplicate stems, zero artificial wrappers
  • 236 Roman numeral questions converted to statement-by-statement explanations
  • Calculation questions now show formula, workings, and final answer
  • Answer-length cueing patched: correct answer uniquely longest below 9%
  • Package size reduced from ~28 MB to ~4.4 MB
